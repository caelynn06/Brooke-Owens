How to play

Walk the map in any direction. Obstacles block the main corridor. The sources that clear them are off to the sides, and not every way in is obvious.

Stand next to an obstacle and pick the source that fits. If the way in is closed, look again.

Keys: arrows or WASD to walk, R to look again, H for a hint, 1 2 3 to use a source.
